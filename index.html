The Person

About Oz

Oziomachi Nwankwo · Oz

Storyteller by instinct.
Craftsperson by practice.

Oziomachi Nwankwo — known simply as Oz — is a Nigerian writer, director, and illustrator working across film, theatre, and visual art. A Best Graduating Student (BGS 5.0), her work is marked by an unflinching curiosity about people, identity, and the African experience.

She has written screenplays, directed stage productions, managed programmes for a beloved West African film festival, and documented the behind-the-scenes of projects she believes in. Wherever she is in the room — in front of a keyboard, on set, or behind a drawing tablet — she is building stories.

Her illustrations carry the same sensibility as her scripts: specific, humane, and rooted in the textures of everyday Nigerian life.
